Intelligent Student Learning Assistant

MEDIUM AI / ML 24 HOURS

Problem Statement

Students often have access to a large amount of educational content but struggle to identify which resources are most suitable for their current knowledge level and learning goals.

Your challenge is to develop an intelligent learning assistant that analyzes student performance and provides personalized recommendations.

Expected Solution

The proposed system should allow students to interact with a learning platform and receive recommendations based on their performance, interests and progress.

  • Student performance tracking
  • Personalized content recommendations
  • Progress monitoring
  • Learning path generation
  • Simple and intuitive dashboard
