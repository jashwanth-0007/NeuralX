01
PARTICIPATION

Who Can Participate?

NeuralX is open to students who are interested in technology, innovation, software development and problem solving.

  • Students from recognized institutions can participate.
  • Participants may come from different academic backgrounds.
  • Teams can consist of students from the same or different institutions, subject to organizer rules.
  • Every participant must provide valid identification details.
02
TEAM FORMATION

Build Your Team.

A strong team combines different skills. Developers, designers, researchers and domain experts are encouraged to collaborate.

  • Each team must have 2 to 4 members.
  • One member must be designated as the team lead.
  • A participant cannot be part of multiple teams.
  • Team names must be unique.
03
DEVELOPMENT

Build During The Event.

Teams are expected to develop their solutions during the designated hackathon period.

  • Teams may use open-source libraries, frameworks and APIs.
  • Previously developed projects should not be submitted as new work.
  • Teams must disclose major third-party components used in their project.
  • All submitted work must belong to the participating team.
04
SUBMISSION

Submit Your Solution.

Each team must submit their project before the official submission deadline.

  • Source code or repository link.
  • Project documentation.
  • Working prototype or demonstration.
  • Short project presentation.
  • Instructions required to run the project.
05
EVALUATION

How Projects Are Judged.

Projects will be evaluated by a panel based on technical quality, innovation, usability and real-world impact.

30% Innovation
25% Technical Quality
20% Impact
15% UI / UX
10% Presentation
06
CONDUCT

Respect The Community.

NeuralX is designed to encourage collaboration, creativity and learning. Every participant is expected to maintain a professional and respectful environment.

  • Respect fellow participants, mentors and organizers.
  • Do not engage in harassment, discrimination or abusive behavior.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt the event infrastructure.
  • Organizers reserve the right to disqualify teams that violate the rules.
